MM Coastal Wealth LLC · FP&A Engine

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026 · Updated October 16, 2026

This policy explains how the FP&A Engine (the "Tool"), an internal tool of MM Coastal Wealth LLC (the "Company"), handles data it receives from QuickBooks Online. It covers this Tool only. For the Company's client privacy notice, see mycoastalwealth.com/privacy-policy.

1. Whose data

The Tool connects only to the Company's own QuickBooks Online account. It does not connect to any other business's QuickBooks account.

2. What data the Tool reads

With read-only access, the Tool may read the Company's accounting data, including:

  • Chart of accounts, journal entries, and transaction details
  • Vendor and customer records (names, balances, and transaction history)
  • Financial reports such as profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, and aging reports
  • Budgets entered in QuickBooks

The Tool does not create, change, or delete any data in QuickBooks.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to produce internal financial statements, dashboards, budget comparisons, and analysis for the Company's finance team and leadership.

4. AI processing

To produce analysis and written commentary, parts of the data may be sent to an AI service provider (currently Anthropic's Claude). This is done only to generate analysis for the Company, under that provider's commercial or account data terms.

5. Where the data is stored

Data retrieved from QuickBooks is stored on Company-controlled computers or Company-controlled systems. QuickBooks access credentials are stored separately from the Tool's code and are never published or shared.

Reports and dashboards reviewed and approved by the CFO, which may include transaction-level detail, may be shared with authorized Company personnel through the AI provider's secure, invitation-only sharing service. Shared reports are stored on that provider's systems and are visible only to the people invited.

6. Who can see it

Access is limited to authorized Company personnel, mainly the finance team and leadership.

7. Sharing

The Company does not sell this data. It is not shared with third parties except the service providers needed to run the Tool (Intuit as the data source, and the AI provider described above, including its invitation-only sharing service), or where required by law or regulation.

8. Retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as needed for the Company's financial reporting and analysis. When the Tool is disconnected from QuickBooks, its stored access credentials are deleted, and stored data can be deleted on request to the contact below.

9. Security

The Company uses reasonable safeguards to protect this data, including read-only access, restricted user access, encrypted storage, and keeping credentials out of source code.

10. Disconnecting

The connection can be ended at any time in QuickBooks Online by going to the connected apps area, selecting the FP&A Engine, and choosing Disconnect.

11. Changes

The Company may update this policy by posting a new version on this page.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: Accountspayable@mycoastalwealth.com

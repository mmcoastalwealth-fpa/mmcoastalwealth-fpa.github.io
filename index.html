FP&A Engine

The FP&A Engine is an internal financial reporting and analysis tool used by the finance team and leadership of MM Coastal Wealth LLC.

It connects to the company's own QuickBooks Online account with read-only access and produces internal financial statements, cash flow and spending dashboards, budget comparisons, and analysis.

The tool is not offered to the public or to other QuickBooks customers.
